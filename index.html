Are these actual machines in stock?

This preview contains example sourcing profiles. They are not live inventory listings. For an actual offer, confirm the model, serial number, year, condition, inspection location and current availability.

Is every machine held in your own warehouse?

The proposed sourcing model works with independent suppliers in China. Machine ownership, supplier location and access for inspection should be disclosed in each offer.

How are price and shipping calculated?

A quotation depends on the exact machine, condition and agreed delivery scope. Freight, local charges and any taxes should be itemized or expressly included in the written quotation.

Can I inspect before confirming an order?

Ask for recent operating videos and nameplate photos. Live video or independent on-site inspection can be included in the requested sourcing plan, subject to supplier agreement.
